LOOKBOOK / IMAGE REFERENCES

01. Vogue Runway - Spring 2026 Menswear index
https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/spring-2026-menswear
Why:
作为当天 lookbook 入口很好，能从 Lemaire、Our Legacy、Wales Bonner、Rick Owens、Hermes 等品牌里继续往下筛，不必依赖素材站。

02. Lemaire Fall 2026 Menswear
https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/fall-2026-menswear/christophe-lemaire
Why:
非常适合你要的 quiet uniform、低调比例、灰幕光影和成熟衣橱感。

03. Lemaire Spring 2026 Menswear
https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/spring-2026-menswear/christophe-lemaire
Why:
更轻一点，适合春夏旅行穿搭参考，重点看宽裤、衬衫、外套层次。

04. Courreges Spring 2026 Menswear
https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/spring-2026-menswear/courreges
Why:
有街头自拍/镜面/未来感，不是完全 JJJJound，但可以给“酷”加一点锋利度。

05. Saint Laurent Spring 2026 Menswear
https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/spring-2026-menswear/saint-laurent
Why:
更狂、更性感、更夜晚。适合做“不要太乖”的调味参考。

06. 032c Spring 2026 Menswear on Vogue
https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows/spring-2026-menswear/032c
Why:
Berlin-coded、冷、带一点拒绝姿态，很适合你的“有态度但不网红”方向。

07. 032c - I WOULD PREFER NOT TO
https://www.032c.com/magazine/i-would-prefer-not-to-032c-readytowear-spring-summer-2026
Why:
lookbook + 文案概念都能用。“passive resistance / I would prefer not to” 这个主题适合做深一点的小红书文案。

VIDEOS / MOVING IMAGE REFERENCES

01. SHOWstudio - Fashion Film Submissions
https://www.showstudio.com/projects/fashion_film_submissions
Why:
比普通品牌 campaign 更野，适合找“狂但有品味”的 moving image 参考。

02. SHOWstudio - Fashion Film Showcase: Summer 2025
https://www.showstudio.com/projects/fashion_film_submissions/fashion-film-showcase-summer-2025
Why:
直接是精选 fashion film，可以从里面找节奏、构图、剪辑和声音参考。

03. SHOWstudio - The Future of Fashion is: Fashion Film
https://www.showstudio.com/projects/fashions-digital-future/the-future-of-fashion-is-fashion-film
Why:
适合理解 fashion film 为什么成立，不只是拿视频当 B-roll。

04. Dazed - A New Generation of Video on Vimeo
https://vimeo.com/channels/dazed
Why:
Dazed 的视频频道，适合找更青年文化、更怪一点的视觉语言。

05. Dazed - The dA-Zed guide to fashion film
https://www.dazeddigital.com/fashion/article/17977/1/the-da-zed-guide-to-fashion-film
Why:
老文章但很有索引价值，可以顺着里面的导演/片名继续挖。

06. Dazed - The most spellbinding fashion films of 2016
https://www.dazeddigital.com/fashion/article/34117/1/the-most-spellbinding-fashion-films-of-2016
Why:
适合找更“疯”的 fashion film 参考，比如舞蹈、怪感、cult celebrity、荒诞广告片结构。

ARTICLES / DEEP READING

01. SSENSE - Culture Predictions for 2026
https://www.ssense.com/en-us/editorial/culture/trend-forecast-2026/
Why:
这篇很适合你账号的底层判断：反 influencer、physical-first、less polished、useless is luxury。能帮助你避开普通旅行博主感。

02. 032c homepage / article index
https://www.032c.com/
Why:
适合定期扫：电影、音乐、互联网、set design、fashion culture 都有，比纯穿搭媒体更有深度。

03. Vogue - Fall 2026, in Designers' Own Words
https://www.vogue.com/article/fall-2026-in-designers-own-words
Why:
可以看设计师自己怎么描述当下情绪，用来训练你的文案语感。

04. Vogue - What Do Fashion People Do All Day?
https://www.vogue.com/article/what-do-fashion-people-do-all-day-answers-from-the-spring-2026-runways
Why:
适合把穿搭和“日常场景”连接起来，不是只讲衣服，而是讲人如何生活。

05. The Guardian - V&A East Museum architecture review
https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2026/apr/15/va-east-architecture-review-from-ceramics-to-codpieces-this-is-a-honey-coloured-treasure-trove-of-human-ingenuity
Why:
建筑、展览、材质、城市文化都能喂给你的 lifestyle 方向，不局限在时装。

06. Vogue - Metropolitan Museum of Art Costume Art exhibition
https://www.vogue.com/article/metropolitan-museum-of-art-costume-art-exhibition
Why:
适合建立“服装作为文化/艺术”的深一点表达，后续可转成小红书读展/看展/穿搭关系类内容。
